    New in KATANA · Shield

    Most challenges fail in one session.
    Shield makes sure yours doesn't.

    Open losses count against the firm's limit before you close them. Shield watches the whole account and acts before the firm does.

    01 · Close all + lock

    Stops the bad day for you

    Daily loss or max drawdown hit, open trades included: every trade on the account closes, pending orders go, trading locks. Even after an MT5 restart.

    02 · Safety buffer

    Stops before the firm does

    KATANA stops you a little before the firm's line, so spread and slippage never break the rule for you.

    03 · Firm presets

    Pick your firm, done

    FundedNext, FTMO, FundingPips and The5ers rules fill in with one tap. Static, trailing or end-of-day drawdown.

    04 · News lock

    No trades into red news

    Reads MT5's own calendar and pauses new trades minutes before and after high-impact news for your pair. A countdown shows on the chart.

    05 · Tilt guard

    No revenge trades

    Done for the day after losses in a row, a short break after a loss, a cap on trades per day, and no bigger lot after a loss.

    06 · Phone alerts

    Know without watching

    Push alerts at 50% and 80% of your limits, before news, on a lock, and when a stop or target is hit.

    New in KATANA 12.10 · Payout rules

    Pass the challenge.
    Then get paid.

    Most firms won't pay out if one day made too much of your profit. KATANA reads your history, shows how much today can still make, and stops new trades before a big day costs you the payout.

    01 · Consistency

    A fourth bar: CONSIST.

    Next to daily loss and drawdown, KATANA shows your score and the room left today. It turns amber, then red, as today fills up.

    02 · Consistency

    Target too big? It shrinks to fit.

    Before the trade goes in, KATANA checks the target. If a win would break the rule, press Yes and the target moves to what today can still make.

    03 · Consistency

    Limit reached. Done for today.

    New trades stop for the day. Trades already open keep running to their stop or target. Your phone gets an alert before you get there.

    04 · R:R lock

    Your plan stays your plan

    Stop and target stay where KATANA placed them. Drag either line in the heat of the moment and it goes straight back.

    05 · Floating loss limit

    Open losses have a ceiling

    Some firms limit how deep open trades can go before you close them. KATANA closes everything before the firm's line.

    06 · Prop or live

    Knows which account you're on

    Prop tools switch on for your challenges and stay out of the way on your own live account. Nothing to set.
