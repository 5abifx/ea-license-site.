    02 · Safety buffer

    Stops before the firm does

    KATANA stops you a little before the firm's line, so spread and slippage never break the rule for you.

    03 · Firm presets

    Pick your firm, done

    FundedNext, FTMO, FundingPips and The5ers rules fill in with one tap. Static, trailing or end-of-day drawdown.

    04 · News lock

    No trades into red news

    Reads MT5's own calendar and pauses new trades minutes before and after high-impact news for your pair. A countdown shows on the chart.

    05 · Tilt guard

    No revenge trades

    Done for the day after losses in a row, a short break after a loss, a cap on trades per day, and no bigger lot after a loss.

    06 · Phone alerts

    Push alerts at 50% and 80% of your limits, before news, on a lock, and when a stop or target is hit.
